Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial project: a small collection of mini games. It has no ads and no paid features. This page explains the little data the site handles.

Who is responsible

The site is run by its author. For any privacy question or request, open an issue at github.com/rciesielski3/mini-game-collection/issues.

What is stored, and where

  • Nickname (the one you type, or a suggested one). It is saved in your browser's local storage and sent with your scores to the leaderboard.
  • Scores and achievements, saved in a Google Firebase database (Cloud Firestore) so the leaderboard works.
  • An anonymous identifier. The site creates a random ID for your browser (stored in local storage and used by Firebase anonymous sign-in) so your scores can be linked to your browser. It contains no name or email.

Nothing else is collected. There is no advertising tracking and no tracking cookies set by this site.

Visitor counter

The site shows one shared number that goes up by one on each visit. It is not linked to any person and no identifier is stored with it.

Why

The data is used only to run the games: to show your scores and achievements and the leaderboard. The legal basis is the performance of the service you choose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Who else handles the data

Google Firebase (Google Cloud) hosts the database and anonymous sign-in and processes the data on the author's behalf. Google may process it on servers outside the European Economic Area, under its own data processing terms.

How long

Scores and the nickname are kept until you ask for them to be deleted, or until the project is shut down. Clearing your browser's site data removes the local nickname and anonymous ID; your scores then stay in the leaderboard unless you ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the data about you, and you can object to its processing. Send the request through the GitHub issues link above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The games are for a general audience. Please do not use your real name or other personal details as a nickname.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.
